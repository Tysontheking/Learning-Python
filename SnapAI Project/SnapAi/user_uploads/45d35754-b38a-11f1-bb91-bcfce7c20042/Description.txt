Ganesh Chaturthi has arrived, and with it, the chant "Ganpati Bappa Morya" fills the air. "Ganpati Bappa Morya, Mangalmurti Morya" is essentially a Marathi devotional chant that enthusiastically welcomes the arrival of Bappa.
